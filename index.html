Call Me

Reminders that call you

Call Me rings like a real phone call at the time you choose and reads your reminder out loud. No internet needed.

Features

  • Rings at the exact minute — even when your phone is locked or asleep.
  • Reads your message aloud with your phone’s offline voices.
  • Optional: ring through your phone’s own dialer and ringtone, and appear in your call log.
  • Repeat daily, on weekdays or weekly. Snooze, missed-call alerts and a call log.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Your reminders never leave your phone.
