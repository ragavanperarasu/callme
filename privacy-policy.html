Call Me

Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

In short
  • Call Me does not collect, share or sell any personal data.
  • There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app has no internet access. Everything stays on your phone.

This policy explains how Call Me (“the app”), developed by Ragavan (“I”, “me”), handles your information. Call Me is an Android app that phones you at a time you choose and reads your reminder out loud.

1. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on your phone, in the app’s private storage:

  • Reminders you create: title, the message to be spoken, date, time and repeat setting.
  • Call history inside the app: whether each reminder call was answered, missed, declined or snoozed, and when.
  • Settings: the first name you enter for greetings (optional), voice, speed, pitch, ring time and similar preferences.

This information is never sent to me or to anyone else. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot transmit data. Android cloud backup is turned off for the app, so your reminders are not copied to a backup service either.

2. Your phone’s call log

If you choose “Ring as a real phone call” and enable Call Me under Calling accounts, Android shows reminders in your phone’s own dialer and records them in your phone’s call log (the reminder title and the time), just like any other call. This record is kept by Android on your device. Call Me cannot read your call log, and you can delete these entries in your Phone app at any time. If you use the “Call Me screen” style instead, nothing is added to the call log.

3. Text-to-speech

Reminders are read aloud by the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone (for example, Speech Services by Google). Call Me only uses voices that are downloaded to the device, so speech is generated on your phone without a network connection.

4. Permissions

PermissionWhy Call Me needs it
NotificationsTo ring and show the incoming reminder call.
Alarms & reminders (exact alarms)To ring at the exact minute you chose, even when the phone is idle.
Full-screen notificationsTo show the call screen over the lock screen, like a real call.
Run at startupTo restore your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.
Vibrate, keep awakeTo vibrate and keep the screen on while a call is ringing.
Calling account (optional)Only if you enable it: lets your phone’s dialer show reminder calls. Call Me cannot make outgoing calls.

Call Me does not request access to your contacts, phone calls or call log, microphone, camera, location, files or the internet.

5. Sharing and selling

No data is shared with or sold to anyone. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

6. Children

Call Me is a general reminder utility and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Deleting your data

All data stays on your phone, so you are always in control of it. See how to delete your data for step-by-step instructions.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app ever needs to collect data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
