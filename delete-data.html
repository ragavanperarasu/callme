Good to know
  • Call Me has no accounts and no servers. Nothing is stored online, so there is no data held by the developer to delete.
  • All your data lives only on your phone, and you can remove it yourself at any time using the steps below.

Delete a single reminder

  1. Open Call Me and tap the reminder.
  2. Tap the trash icon at the top right.
  3. Tap Delete. The reminder and its scheduled call are removed immediately.

Clear the in-app call log

  1. Open Call Me and switch to the Call log tab.
  2. Tap Clear, then confirm.

Delete everything

Either of these removes all reminders, call history and settings permanently:

  • Clear storage: Settings → Apps → Call Me → Storage → Clear storage (on Samsung: Clear data).
  • Uninstall: long-press the Call Me icon → Uninstall.

Reminder calls in your phone’s call log

If you used “Ring as a real phone call”, Android also lists reminder calls in your phone’s call log. To remove them, open your Phone app → Recents, then long-press an entry and choose Delete (or select several to delete them together).
